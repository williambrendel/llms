# What Nereus Covers

## Executive Summary

Nereus is designed for any facility that operates a water treatment system, covering open recirculating cooling towers, closed loop chilled water, hot water loops, boilers, wastewater, and humidification systems, plus Legionella prevention and control aligned with ASHRAE 188.

## Overview

The platform spans the full set of water treatment system types facility teams typically operate — not just cooling towers in isolation. A single data center, hospital, or industrial plant usually runs four or five of these system types simultaneously, and Nereus is built to handle all of them through one unified interface rather than fragmenting monitoring across separate tools.

## Facility Types Served

Nereus is designed for any facility that operates a water treatment system, including:

- Data centers
- Industrial plants
- Commercial buildings
- Hospitals
- Hotels
- And other facility types that run cooling, heating, or water treatment infrastructure

Whether the operation is a single building or a multi-site portfolio, the same platform handles it.

## Open Recirculating Cooling Towers

Open recirculating cooling towers are the most common water treatment surface in industrial facilities, and Nereus provides the broadest coverage for them. The platform addresses scaling, corrosion, biocide programs, cycles of concentration, Langelier Saturation Index, and blowdown management.

Operators get guidance on the full operational lifecycle: setting cycles, controlling blowdown, selecting biocides, interpreting saturation indices, diagnosing scale and corrosion, and responding to biological events. Both the chemistry side (treatment program design) and the operational side (cycles, blowdown, drift) are covered.

## Closed Loop Chilled Water Systems

For closed loop chilled water systems, Nereus covers corrosion inhibitor programs, chemistry management, and biological control. The platform addresses inhibitor selection (nitrite, molybdate, sulfite-based), reserve monitoring, makeup water tracking, and the operational practices specific to closed loops — including why tight loops should show near-zero makeup water usage and how to diagnose when they don't.

## Hot Water Loops and Hydronic Systems

For hot water loops, Nereus covers corrosion inhibitors, glycol management, pH control, and closed hydronic system protocols. Glycol concentration, inhibitor reserve, oxygen scavenging, and pH maintenance all fall within the platform's scope.

## Boiler Systems

For boiler systems — both steam and hot water — Nereus covers chemistry programs, scale and corrosion prevention, blowdown management, and steam and hot water boiler protocols. Boiler water chemistry, feedwater treatment, condensate return management, and blowdown optimization are addressed through the same interface as the rest of the facility's water systems.

## Wastewater Systems

For wastewater, Nereus covers chemistry management, compliance, and treatment programs for industrial wastewater systems. Operators handling industrial discharge compliance get guidance on chemistry programs and reporting requirements alongside the rest of the facility's water management.

## Humidification Systems

For humidification systems, Nereus covers ultrasonic precision humidification monitoring and chemistry. The platform tracks humidification water quality alongside cooling and process water in the same unified view.

## Legionella Prevention and Control

Nereus covers Legionella prevention and control as a distinct vertical that spans multiple system types. The platform provides water management plan guidance, biocide selection, temperature control, and monitoring protocols aligned with ASHRAE 188 standards.

For facilities subject to ASHRAE 188, this includes building the water management plan itself, defining risk hierarchies across cooling towers, domestic hot water, and decorative fountains, selecting culture vs qPCR sampling approaches, setting response thresholds, and executing remediation procedures including hyperchlorination when needed.

## Key Takeaways

If your facility runs cooling towers, closed loops, hot water, boilers, wastewater, or humidifiers — or any combination — Nereus is built to cover it in one platform. Legionella management cuts across multiple system types and is aligned with ASHRAE 188 standards.
