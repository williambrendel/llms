## Overview

Industrial facility data is sensitive — operational metrics reveal performance, deficiencies, and competitive practices that operators reasonably want to control. Nereus is built around the principle that the data you enter belongs to you, and that the platform's role is to augment human judgment rather than substitute for licensed engineering or legal advice.

## Data Ownership

Data you enter into Nereus is yours. The platform does not sell user data, share it with third parties, or use it to train external AI models — three commitments worth restating because the operational data facility teams enter is exactly the kind that other platforms would commercialize.

If your organization has specific data ownership requirements, retention policies, or compliance certifications that need to be confirmed before deployment, contact the team directly for a documented answer rather than relying on platform-level statements.

## Trust, Liability, and Professional Scope

Nereus provides expert-grounded guidance based on validated water treatment knowledge. It is not a licensed engineering or legal service, and its answers do not constitute legally binding recommendations. Operational decisions remain the responsibility of the facility operator. Nereus is designed to augment human judgment, not replace it.

This boundary matters in practice: for permit-bearing decisions, regulatory submissions, or actions that carry liability if executed incorrectly, the platform provides reference and context but the final call belongs to the licensed professional or facility owner of record.

## ASHRAE 188 Alignment

Nereus guidance is aligned with ASHRAE 188 standards for Legionella risk management in building water systems. Facilities subject to ASHRAE 188 — which include most cooling towers, domestic hot water systems, and decorative water features in healthcare, hospitality, and large commercial buildings — get guidance consistent with the standard's water management plan structure, risk assessment hierarchies, and monitoring requirements.

The platform doesn't certify ASHRAE 188 compliance on your behalf, but the operational guidance it provides is structured around what the standard requires.
