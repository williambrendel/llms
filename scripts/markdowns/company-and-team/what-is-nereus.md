# What Is Nereus

## Executive Summary

Nereus is an AI platform that gives facility teams operational intelligence for water and energy systems through two integrated capabilities: Sentinel, which detects anomalies and predicts emerging risks, and Ask Nereus, which explains causes and guides remediation using expert knowledge.

## Overview

Most data center water treatment failures don't happen because data is missing — they happen because critical changes go undetected until systems are already outside safe operating conditions. Facilities collect daily test results but rarely analyze them in any meaningful way, leaving facility staff to spend disproportionate time chasing water-related issues that drive hidden losses across electricity, water, sewer, and equipment costs. The name Nereus comes from Greek mythology — the primordial sea god known as the "Old Man of the Sea," revered for foresight, clarity, and the ability to adapt. That's the role the platform plays for water and energy operations.

## The Problem Nereus Solves

Water treatment in industrial facilities has long operated on instinct, habit, and vendor relationships rather than data science. Operators collect test results, but those results get recorded and filed without any system to interpret them, flag problems, or quantify their financial impact. The core issue isn't a lack of data. It's the absence of intelligence around that data.

The consequences compound. Without proper control of scale, corrosion, and microbial growth, facilities experience equipment degradation, excess energy consumption, unnecessary water waste, and elevated health risks including *Legionella*. Facility staff end up spending disproportionate time managing water issues at the expense of their broader responsibilities, creating operational drag and hidden financial losses that rarely get traced back to their true cause.

When problems escalate, the typical response is to switch water treatment vendors or bring in outside consultants. But vendors offer similar basic services without deep analytics, and consultants deliver one-time assessments rather than continuous intelligence. Both approaches share a fundamental reliance on human judgment over data, and the cycle repeats.

## The Platform: Two Intelligence Systems Working Together

Nereus combines predictive monitoring with expert operational reasoning. The platform is built around two integrated intelligence systems that work in real time:

**Nereus Sentinel** — the detection and predictive intelligence layer. Sentinel continuously monitors water chemistry, cooling performance, and facility telemetry using statistical analysis, physics-based modeling, and chemistry-aware intelligence. It detects subtle anomalies, predicts emerging risks, and identifies operational drift before it becomes downtime, asset damage, compliance exposure, or unnecessary cost. From abnormal conductivity trends to declining heat-transfer efficiency, Sentinel transforms raw operational data into actionable foresight.

**Ask Nereus** — the reasoning and knowledge intelligence layer. Ask Nereus is a conversational AI assistant grounded in the Water Dr. technical archive, providing instant access to expert guidance on scale, corrosion, biological control, system drift, and remediation strategies. It functions like a senior water treatment consultant available 24/7, surfacing the next logical troubleshooting steps through contextual Anticipated Questions, and escalating to human specialists when situations demand on-site judgment.

Together, the two systems close the loop: Sentinel sees the problem coming, Ask Nereus explains what to do about it.

## How Nereus Compares

### Nereus vs. General AI

General-purpose AI models like ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini are trained on broad internet data and are prone to hallucination — generating confident but incorrect answers. Nereus is grounded in a proprietary, expert-validated knowledge base specific to industrial water treatment. For something as critical as cooling system chemistry, the distinction between "sounds right" and "is right" matters.

### Nereus vs. Water Treatment Consultants

Nereus complements consultants rather than replacing them. Consultants handle complex, site-specific challenges that require physical presence and judgment. Nereus fills the gaps between visits, answering the questions that come up at 10pm on a Tuesday when no one is available. For situations that genuinely need on-site expertise, Ask Nereus escalates directly to the Water Dr. team.

### Nereus vs. Chemical Vendors

Chemical suppliers offer product-specific support tied to their own product lines. Nereus provides vendor-neutral answers grounded in water treatment science, making it a valuable independent reference alongside your supplier relationship.

## Key Takeaways

Nereus is built for facility teams who already have data but lack the intelligence layer to act on it. Two integrated systems do the work: Sentinel catches operational drift before alarms would normally trigger, and Ask Nereus explains what the drift means and how to fix it. Together they replace the cycle of switching vendors and waiting for consultant visits with continuous, data-driven decision support.
