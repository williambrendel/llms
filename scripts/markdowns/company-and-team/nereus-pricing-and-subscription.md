# Nereus Pricing and Subscription

## Executive Summary

Nereus is a paid SaaS platform with pricing handled directly by the Water Dr. team rather than published on the platform. A free trial may be available, and cancellation policy and contract terms are handled case-by-case. To get pricing, ask about a trial, or discuss cancellation, contact grant@yourwaterdr.com or call +1 (925) 470-0491.

## Overview

Nereus serves facilities of widely varying sizes — single-building operators alongside multi-site enterprises — so pricing is structured around the actual scope of each deployment rather than a fixed published rate. The team prefers a direct conversation to understand what's being deployed before quoting plans and rates.

## Pricing

Nereus is a paid SaaS platform. The platform is not free to use, and an account is required to access it.

Pricing details — including current plan rates, available subscription tiers, and whether monthly or annual billing options are offered — are provided directly by the team and not published on the platform. To get pricing information or discuss billing options, contact grant@yourwaterdr.com or call +1 (925) 470-0491.

## Free Trial

A free trial may be available. Contact the team to ask about trial access before committing to a paid subscription. The team prefers to have a conversation about the use case before opening a trial, so reach out with a brief description of the facility or operation you'd be evaluating Nereus against.

## Cancellation Policy and Contract Terms

Cancellation policy and minimum contract commitment terms are handled directly by the team. There is no standard self-service cancellation flow — cancellations, contract amendments, and term-related questions all go through Grant.
