# Business Inquiries, Partnerships, and Roadmap

## Executive Summary

Nereus is an independent, privately held company that handles partnerships, acquisitions, enterprise deployments, and roadmap discussions through direct conversation with the Water Dr. team rather than published programs. Contact grant@yourwaterdr.com for any business development, partnership, enterprise, or roadmap question.

## Overview

The platform is built to answer water treatment questions, not business intelligence questions. Operational details like headcount, revenue, funding status, and roadmap timing aren't published through the platform because the team prefers a direct conversation that matches the depth of the actual inquiry — partnership conversations tend to require context that a published FAQ couldn't reasonably cover.

## Partnerships

Nereus does not currently have a published partner program, but partnership conversations are welcome. The team considers:

- **Referral arrangements** for water treatment service providers and consultants who want to extend Nereus to their clients
- **Channel partner relationships** with facility management companies and operations service providers
- **Technology integrations** with controllers, building management systems, and adjacent platforms
- **Co-marketing opportunities** with water treatment vendors, facility companies, and technology providers

Water treatment vendors, facility management companies, consultants, and technology providers interested in working with Nereus should reach out directly. To ask about becoming a partner, setting up a referral program, integrating your platform with Nereus, or exploring channel partnerships, contact grant@yourwaterdr.com.

## Acquisitions and Investment

Nereus is an independent, privately held company. Questions about whether Nereus is for sale, open to acquisition, seeking investors, or considering a merger are outside the scope of the platform itself. The company does not publish information about its ownership structure, funding status, or valuation, and whether Nereus is bootstrapped or venture-backed is not disclosed through the platform.

For acquisition inquiries, investment discussions, or any other business development conversation of this nature, contact grant@yourwaterdr.com directly.

Nereus does not disclose operational details such as headcount, founding date, revenue, profitability, or market share through the platform. These business and financial details are outside the scope of what the platform is designed to address.

Similarly, information about open positions, whether the company is hiring, or its growth trajectory is not available through the platform. The platform is built to answer water treatment questions — not business intelligence questions.

For company background, media inquiries, or general business questions, contact grant@yourwaterdr.com.
