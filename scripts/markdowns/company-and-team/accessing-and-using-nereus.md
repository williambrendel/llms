# Accessing and Using Nereus

## Executive Summary

Nereus runs in any desktop browser at asknereus.com without installation, with a fully responsive mobile experience optimized for iOS and a native app available in the mobile store. An account is required to access the platform, and an active internet connection is required at all times — Nereus is a live platform and does not operate offline.

## Overview

The platform was designed to be as intuitive and conversational as modern AI tools, requiring no specialized technical training to navigate. Beyond day-to-day facility operations, Nereus also serves as a practical resource for onboarding new technicians and a credible reference operators can pull up during client meetings to back a recommendation with expert knowledge.

## Devices and Browsers

Nereus is accessible via desktop browser at asknereus.com on any operating system — no installation required. The web application works across multiple browser tabs simultaneously without conflict, so operators can run different views, conversations, or facility dashboards side by side.

For mobile use, the platform is fully responsive and optimized for iOS devices. A native app is also available from your mobile store for the full experience, useful for field operators who prefer a dedicated app over a browser session.

## Internet Connection Requirement

Nereus requires an active internet connection to function. It is a live platform — pulling real-time facility data, running predictive analytics, and querying the verified knowledge base — and cannot operate in an offline environment. Field operators working in areas with intermittent connectivity should plan around this constraint.

## Account Setup

An account is required to access the Nereus platform. To get started, contact the Water Dr. team and they will create an account for you. The platform is designed to be self-explanatory — once your account is active, just start asking questions and exploring the interface.

For password resets, account recovery, login issues, or profile updates, contact the team directly via email or phone. Account-related requests are handled by the team rather than through a self-service portal.

## Interface Design

The interface is built around a conversational pattern comparable in ease of use to everyday AI assistants. Operators at any skill level can interact with complex facility data through a clean, guided experience — there's no specialized training required, no command syntax to memorize, and no dashboards to configure before getting useful answers.

## Sharing and Conversation History

Individual question-and-answer responses can be shared directly from the platform, which is useful for forwarding a specific recommendation to a colleague, attaching evidence to a maintenance log, or including expert guidance in a service report. Full conversation history export is not currently available — sharing happens at the per-answer level.

## Additional Uses

Beyond day-to-day operations, Nereus serves two specific roles worth highlighting:

**Staff training and operator education** — Nereus is a practical resource for onboarding new technicians. New hires can ask questions and get verified, expert-validated answers rather than relying on senior operators to explain everything from scratch. The platform's conversational interface fits naturally into informal training sessions.

**Client-facing and professional use** — Operators and consultants can pull up Nereus in a meeting to answer a client question on the spot or support a recommendation with expert knowledge. Because answers are vendor-neutral and grounded in the Water Dr. technical archive rather than internet data, they hold up under scrutiny.

## Key Takeaways

Access Nereus at asknereus.com on desktop or via the mobile app on iOS. Contact the team to set up an account before first use, and plan for an internet connection on-site since the platform doesn't work offline. For training new technicians or supporting client meetings, the platform is a practical reference you can pull up on the spot to back a recommendation with expert knowledge.
