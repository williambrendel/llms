## The Anatomy of Filtration Failure

The industry standard for Direct-to-Chip (DTC) cooling creates a fundamental mismatch between biological realities and physical specifications.

**The Micron Mismatch**

High-performance cold plates for NVIDIA B200 and AMD MI300X class processors feature micro-channel fins spaced at 100–500 microns. Most CDUs utilize 50-micron strainers that stop large debris but allow massive contamination spectrums to pass through. Viruses measure 0.01–0.3μm and bacteria span 0.5–15μm, making them invisible to conventional filtration until they colonize and clog the chip pathways.

**The Dead vs. Alive Problem**

Traditional UV or chemical dosing systems create a secondary crisis called "Necromass." Killing bacteria without sub-micron filtration leaves dead biological matter that forms sticky sludge more thermally insulating than live bacteria. Only absolute filtration removes both living and dead biology to maintain pristine thermal interfaces.
