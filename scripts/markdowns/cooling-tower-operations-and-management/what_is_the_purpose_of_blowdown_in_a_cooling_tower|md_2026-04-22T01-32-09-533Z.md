# What is the purpose of blowdown in a cooling tower?

## Executive Summary

Blowdown controls water chemistry by removing concentrated recirculating water that has accumulated dissolved and suspended impurities, preventing scale formation, corrosion, fouling, foaming, and carryover.

## Overview

Cooling towers face a relentless chemistry problem: every gallon of water that evaporates leaves all its dissolved minerals behind. Without intervention, these concentrating impurities would quickly destroy the system through scaling and corrosion.

## How Concentration Builds Up

Water evaporates from cooling towers continuously, but the dissolved solids stay behind in the recirculating water. This process concentrates everything dissolved in the original makeup water—TDS, conductivity, calcium, magnesium, chloride, and silica all increase over time.

The concentration effect creates a cascade of operational problems. Scale formation occurs when minerals like calcium and magnesium reach saturation levels and precipitate onto heat transfer surfaces. Corrosion accelerates as chloride concentrations rise. Fouling develops when suspended solids accumulate. Foaming disrupts normal tower operation, and carryover wastes water and chemicals while potentially damaging downstream equipment.

## How Blowdown Solves the Problem

Blowdown breaks the concentration cycle by deliberately discharging a portion of the concentrated recirculating water. The system replaces this discharged water with fresh makeup water that has lower TDS levels.

This continuous removal and replacement process maintains water chemistry within acceptable operating ranges. The blowdown rate determines how concentrated the tower water becomes—more blowdown means lower concentration but higher water usage, while less blowdown conserves water but allows higher concentration levels.

## Key Takeaway

Monitor TDS or conductivity levels daily and maintain blowdown rates that keep concentration ratios between 3:1 and 5:1 compared to makeup water. Consider automated blowdown controllers that maintain consistent water chemistry without manual intervention.
