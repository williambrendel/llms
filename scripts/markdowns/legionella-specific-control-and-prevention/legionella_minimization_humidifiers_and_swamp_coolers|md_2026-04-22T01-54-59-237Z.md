# How Do I Safely Maintain Humidifiers and Swamp Coolers for Legionella Control?

## Executive Summary

Empty and clean humidifier tanks daily, and drain-clean-dry swamp coolers before every season. For industrial applications with persistent biofilm, EPA-registered DBNPA biocides offer additional control when used according to label requirements.

## Overview

A single neglected humidifier tank or stagnant swamp cooler sump can harbor millions of Legionella bacteria within days. While the CDC doesn't mandate specific testing frequencies for these devices, they establish clear operational controls that prevent most problems before they start. The most effective approach combines simple daily maintenance with strategic seasonal procedures.

## Humidifier Control Requirements

### Testing Protocol

The CDC does not prescribe routine Legionella testing frequency for humidifiers. Testing decisions fall under Water Management Program (WMP) authority based on risk assessment and operational needs.

### Daily Maintenance

The CDC establishes one non-negotiable requirement: **"Empty and clean tanks on humidifiers daily."** This prevents the stagnant water conditions that allow Legionella multiplication.

### Seasonal Operations

**Shutdown procedures:** Drain completely, clean all wetted surfaces, and dry thoroughly. Never store humidifiers with standing water.

**Restart procedures:** Clean first using manufacturer instructions as the minimum standard, then refill and verify proper operation before returning to service.

## Swamp Cooler Management

### Risk Factors

OSHA identifies intermittent use as the primary risk factor for evaporative air coolers. When units sit idle, stagnant water accumulates in sumps and creates ideal conditions for Legionella growth. Cleaning prior to use disrupts this cycle.

### Pre-Season Startup

Every seasonal restart requires three critical steps:

1. **Drain** all stagnant water from sump and reservoir
2. **Physically clean** sump, pan, and wetted surfaces to remove slime, scale, and debris
3. **Verify** drainage and airflow function properly

The CDC emphasizes managed airflow across condensate pans as essential for evaporative cooler operation.

### In-Season Maintenance

Keep sump and pan free of dirt, debris, and biofilm. Avoid prolonged stagnant water conditions through regular operation or drainage during extended idle periods.

### Post-Season Shutdown

Drain and dry completely. Standing water during storage creates the worst-case scenario for bacterial growth.

## DBNPA Biocide Applications

### Industrial Use Cases

DBNPA (2,2-dibromo-3-nitrilopropionamide) serves as a fast-acting non-oxidizing biocide in industrial recirculating water cooling systems, including cooling towers, evaporative condensers, and air washers.

### Critical Safety Constraints

Three non-negotiable requirements govern DBNPA use:

1. **EPA registration verification:** Use only products registered for your exact application and follow label instructions precisely
2. **Aerosol safety:** Never use DBNPA where treated water can aerosolize into occupied spaces unless the product label explicitly permits it and industrial hygiene review approves
3. **Hazard management:** DBNPA causes skin and eye irritation, sensitization, and aquatic toxicity, requiring proper handling and discharge controls

### Implementation Strategy

Treat DBNPA as an optional "event-based" control for systems with persistent slime, biofouling, or repeated loss of control. Implementation requires:

- Unit design that supports safe dosing without aerosol exposure
- Compliance with discharge permits and environmental constraints  
- Product label coverage for the specific application

DBNPA supplements but never replaces the primary controls of drain-clean-dry procedures and stagnant water prevention.

## Operational Takeaways

Implement daily tank cleaning for humidifiers and complete seasonal drain-clean-dry cycles for swamp coolers. Test water systems when operational changes occur or control measures fail, not according to arbitrary schedules. For industrial facilities with recurring biofilm problems, evaluate EPA-registered DBNPA products designed for your specific cooling system type, ensuring proper ventilation prevents aerosol exposure to occupied areas.
