# What's the maximum silica level Acumer 5000 can handle in cooling towers?

## Executive Summary

Acumer 5000 can handle up to 150 ppm of soluble silica in cooling tower systems, though it works primarily as a dispersant rather than a dedicated silica stabilizer.

## Overview

Most cooling tower operators worry about calcium and phosphate scaling, but silica presents a different challenge entirely. At just 150 ppm, dissolved silica can form glass-hard deposits that resist chemical cleaning and destroy heat transfer efficiency. Acumer 5000 tackles this problem not by preventing silica precipitation, but by keeping the particles dispersed before they can cement onto heat exchange surfaces.

## What is Acumer 5000?

Acumer 5000 is a polyacrylate-based multifunctional copolymer developed by Dow Chemical, now part of DuPont's water treatment portfolio. This polymer serves dual roles as both a dispersant and antiscalant in cooling water systems.

The polymer prevents scaling through two mechanisms: threshold inhibition and crystal distortion. It interferes with crystal growth of calcium phosphate, calcium carbonate, and magnesium silicate while simultaneously dispersing suspended solids and iron oxides. This dual action enhances system cleanliness, improves heat transfer, and extends equipment life.

Cooling tower operators typically find Acumer 5000 blended with other scale and corrosion inhibitors in formulated treatment products rather than as a standalone chemical.

## Silica Control Capabilities

Acumer 5000 was not specifically designed as a silica stabilizer, but it demonstrates effectiveness in enhancing silica dispersancy, particularly when magnesium silicate scale threatens system performance.

**What it measures:** The maximum concentration of dissolved silica that remains manageable in cooling systems

**Silica Tolerance Limit:**
```
Maximum soluble silica = 150 ppm (as SiO₂)
```

**Example:** A cooling tower operating at 4 cycles of concentration with makeup water containing 35 ppm silica would reach 140 ppm silica in the circulating water—right at the practical limit for Acumer 5000's effectiveness.

The actual silica tolerance depends heavily on other system conditions including pH, magnesium concentration, and operating temperature. Higher magnesium levels reduce silica tolerance because magnesium silicate forms more readily than pure silica deposits.

Acumer 5000 helps disperse colloidal silica and inhibits magnesium silicate fouling, which becomes problematic at higher cycles of concentration or when treating high-silica makeup waters.

## Dosage Requirements

Acumer 5000 requires careful dosing to achieve optimal performance. The typical dosage range for the active polymer in cooling towers spans 5 to 30 ppm, with the specific dose determined by several factors.

Water chemistry drives dosage requirements more than any other factor. Systems with high alkalinity, hardness, or silica content require higher doses. The desired cycles of concentration also influences dosing—operators pushing for higher cycles to conserve water need more polymer to handle the concentrated contaminants.

System temperature and flow rates affect polymer performance. Higher temperatures accelerate scaling reactions, requiring increased dosage. Poor flow distribution creates dead zones where scaling accelerates, again demanding higher treatment levels.

Systems prone to heavy scaling or those treating poor-quality makeup water typically require the higher end of the dosage range at 20-30 ppm active polymer.

## Synergies with PBTC

Acumer 5000 demonstrates strong synergies when combined with PBTC (2-Phosphonobutane-1,2,4-tricarboxylic acid). This combination creates a more robust treatment program than either chemical alone.

PBTC excels as a threshold inhibitor and corrosion inhibitor, particularly for iron and steel components. When paired with Acumer 5000, the combination provides complementary scale inhibition—Acumer handles dispersancy and particulate scale while PBTC tackles calcium phosphate and provides corrosion protection.

The Acumer 5000/PBTC combination offers enhanced thermal stability compared to older phosphonates like HEDP. This stability matters in high-temperature cooling applications where chemical breakdown can leave systems unprotected.

The pairing also reduces phosphate loading in the system discharge, which helps operators meet increasingly strict environmental compliance requirements. Formulators often combine these chemicals in low-zinc or no-zinc formulations to control both scale and corrosion in open recirculating cooling systems.

## Key Takeaways

Monitor silica levels weekly in your cooling tower, keeping soluble silica below 150 ppm when using Acumer 5000. Consider combining Acumer 5000 with PBTC at 15-25 ppm total active ingredients for systems with challenging water chemistry. When silica consistently exceeds 150 ppm, evaluate alternative technologies like side-stream filtration or precipitation softening to reduce makeup water silica before it enters your cooling system.
