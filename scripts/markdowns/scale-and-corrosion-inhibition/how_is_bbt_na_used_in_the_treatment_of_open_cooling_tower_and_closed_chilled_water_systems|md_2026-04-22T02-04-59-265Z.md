# How is BBT.Na used in the treatment of open cooling tower and closed chilled water systems?

## Executive Summary

Sodium Butyl Benzotriazole (BBT.Na) protects copper and brass components in cooling systems by forming a molecular barrier film at 1-5 ppm dosages, preventing corrosion while remaining stable in harsh water conditions.

## Overview

A single copper heat exchanger tube failure can cost $50,000 in emergency repairs and downtime. BBT.Na represents a significant advance over traditional benzotriazole inhibitors, offering superior protection in the oxidizing environments and high-chloride waters that destroy conventional copper corrosion inhibitors within weeks.

## What Is Sodium Butyl Benzotriazole

Sodium Butyl Benzotriazole belongs to the benzotriazole family of copper corrosion inhibitors. The butyl group modification enhances the molecule's stability and protective capabilities compared to standard benzotriazole (BZT). The sodium salt form provides high water solubility, making it ideal for water treatment applications.

## Protection Mechanisms in Cooling Systems

BBT.Na adsorbs onto copper alloy surfaces, creating a thin, invisible molecular film. This protective barrier blocks oxygen, chlorides, and other corrosive agents from reaching the metal surface. The inhibitor prevents two critical failure modes: pitting corrosion that creates pinhole leaks, and galvanic corrosion between copper components and steel in mixed-metal systems.

The butyl modification provides superior stability in oxidizing environments compared to traditional benzotriazole. Standard BZT degrades rapidly when exposed to chlorine or bromine biocides, but BBT.Na maintains its protective film longer under these harsh conditions.

## Application Parameters and Dosing

| Parameter | Value / Range |
| --- | --- |
| Target dosage | 1–5 ppm active BBT |
| pH stability | Effective between 6.5–9.5 |
| Metal protection | Primarily copper/brass |
| Solubility | High (due to sodium salt form) |

BBT.Na works effectively across alkaline pH ranges of 7-9+, which covers typical cooling tower operating conditions. The inhibitor maintains stability in high-chloride and high-TDS water, making it suitable for facilities using reclaimed water or operating at high concentration cycles.

## Synergistic Combinations

BBT.Na combines effectively with other water treatment chemicals. Operators typically pair it with PBTC or phosphonates for steel corrosion protection, polyacrylates for dispersion control, and zinc or molybdate for enhanced metal protection. The inhibitor maintains compatibility with oxidizing biocides, though sustained high biocide levels can degrade the protective film over extended periods.

## Key Advantages

BBT.Na reduces copper leaching into system discharge, helping facilities meet increasingly strict discharge permit limits for copper. The stronger protective film and longer-lasting protection reduce maintenance frequency compared to regular BZT or tolyltriazole (TTA). This enhanced performance proves especially valuable in reclaim water systems and high-chloride environments where traditional inhibitors fail rapidly.

## Practical Implementation

Monitor copper corrosion rates monthly using corrosion coupons, maintain BBT.Na levels between 2-4 ppm for most systems, and consider upgrading from standard benzotriazole when copper discharge limits drop below 1.0 mg/L or when using oxidizing biocides regularly.
